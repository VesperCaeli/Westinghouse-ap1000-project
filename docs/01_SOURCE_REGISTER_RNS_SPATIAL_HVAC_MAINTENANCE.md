# Public Source Register Supplement — RNS Spatial / HVAC / Electrical / Maintenance Reconstruction

This supplement extends the RNS reconstruction beyond process topology. Under the project completeness rule, an RNS room or loop is not complete until its physical enclosure, access/removal paths, permanent handling hardware, HVAC/ventilation, chilled-water interfaces, electrical/raceway infrastructure, instrumentation/control interfaces, drainage, fire protection, radiation-monitoring interfaces and maintainability provisions are either reconstructed or explicitly left open.

## Configuration-control rules

- A room number proves a spatial container, not its complete floor plan.
- A work-package title proves a retrieval route and work scope, not the final as-built geometry.
- Generic AP1000/UK-GDA design evidence is never silently promoted to Vogtle as-built evidence.
- HVAC fan, coil, chilled-water, electrical-source and control chains remain separate objects until an explicit tag-to-tag bridge is recovered.
- Maintenance provisions such as hatches, padeyes, monorails, disassembly tables, laydown areas and removal clearances are mandatory loop objects rather than optional architectural notes.
- Room completion requires explicit disposition of doors/hatches, corridors, stairs/ladders, floor/wall/ceiling penetrations, drains/sumps, lighting/receptacles, fire detection/suppression/barriers, radiation-monitoring interfaces, HVAC supply/return paths, chilled-water piping, electrical/raceways, instrument/control wiring, structural supports and maintenance/removal routes.
