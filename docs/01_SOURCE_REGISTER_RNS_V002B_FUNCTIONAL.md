| SRC-0474 | A | AP1000-R19-CERTIFIED | Westinghouse AP1000 Design Control Document, Revision 19, Tier 2, Table 6.2.3-1, *Containment Mechanical Penetrations and Isolation Valves* | DCD Rev.19 Chapter 6, Table 6.2.3-1 Sheets 2 and 4 | Certified containment-isolation configuration for `RNS-PL-V002A/B` on the RCS-to-RHR-pump line: position sequence **C-O-C** for normal/shutdown/post-accident; closure signals **HR** (high containment radiation) and **S** (safety injection); closure time `std`, defined by the table as the industry standard for the valve type, **<=60 seconds**. | SRC-0475 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial FSER, Chapter 5, *Reactor Coolant System and Connected Systems*, RNS ISLOCA design evaluation | Chapter 5, discussion of DCD Tier 2 §5.4.7.2.2, p.5-84 | NRC states that the motor-operated RNS isolation valves connected to the RCS hot leg are interlocked to prevent opening at RCS pressure above **3.21 MPa (450 psig)**. They are also interlocked against opening unless the isolation valve from the IRWST to the RNS pump suction header is **closed**. Power to these isolation valves is administratively blocked at the valve motor control center to prevent inadvertent opening. The RNS also provides suction-line pressure indication and a high-pressure alarm in the MCR. This resolves the AP1000 design-level opening-permissive threshold and the functional IRWST-path permissive, but the initial-FSER source predates later Revision-19 implementation and therefore does not by itself identify current Unit-3 PMS point addresses, pressure-transmitter tag/channel, relay logic, MCC cubicle, or exact IRWST isolation-valve tag population. Reconcile implementation against `APP-PMS-J1-119`, Unit-3 CIM testing and current wiring. |
| SRC-0476 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-I&C-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial FSER, Chapter 7, §7.6.1, *Normal Residual Heat Removal System Isolation Valves* | Chapter 7 §7.6.1, p.7-59 | NRC describes the inner/outer RNS suction-valve interlock architecture: the valves are normally closed; operator opening is prevented unless RCS pressure is below a preset value and the applicable IRWST suction/discharge valves are closed; the two series valves in each of two parallel hot-leg suction paths use identical logic, while the pressure transmitter used for the inner-valve interlocks is **diverse** from the transmitter used for the outer-valve interlocks. The valves may be closed from the MCR at any time; during extended RNS operation after cooldown their motor breakers are opened/removed to prevent inadvertent closure, and MCR/remote-shutdown alarms indicate RCS pressure above RNS design pressure after the valves are open. The same historical passage says the four MOVs were powered from 125-Vdc safety-grade buses; that voltage statement is **superseded** by the later 125→250-Vdc design change controlled in `SRC-0467`/CHG-009 and must not be used for current Unit-3 source voltage. Use this source for interlock topology/diversity/operational-control history only pending current Unit-3 logic reconciliation. |
