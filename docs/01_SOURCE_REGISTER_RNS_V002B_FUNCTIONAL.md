| SRC-0475 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial FSER, Chapter 5, *Reactor Coolant System and Connected Systems*, RNS ISLOCA design evaluation | Chapter 5, discussion of DCD Tier 2 §5.4.7.2.2, p.5-84 | NRC states that the motor-operated RNS isolation valves connected to the RCS hot leg are interlocked to prevent opening at RCS pressure above **3.21 MPa (450 psig)**. They are also interlocked against opening unless the isolation valve from the IRWST to the RNS pump suction header is **closed**. Power to these isolation valves is administratively blocked at the valve motor control center to prevent inadvertent opening. The RNS also provides suction-line pressure indication and a high-pressure alarm in the MCR. This resolves the AP1000 design-level opening-permissive threshold and the functional IRWST-path permissive, but the initial-FSER source predates later Revision-19 implementation and therefore does not by itself identify current Unit-3 PMS point addresses, pressure-transmitter tag/channel, relay logic, MCC cubicle, or exact IRWST isolation-valve tag population. Reconcile implementation against `APP-PMS-J1-119`, Unit-3 CIM testing and current wiring. |
| SRC-0476 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-I&C-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial FSER, Chapter 7, §7.6.1, *Normal Residual Heat Removal System Isolation Valves* | Chapter 7 §7.6.1, p.7-59 | NRC describes the inner/outer RNS suction-valve interlock architecture: the valves are normally closed; operator opening is prevented unless RCS pressure is below a preset value and the applicable IRWST suction/discharge valves are closed; the two series valves in each of two parallel hot-leg suction paths use identical logic, while the pressure transmitter used for the inner-valve interlocks is **diverse** from the transmitter used for the outer-valve interlocks. The valves may be closed from the MCR at any time; during extended RNS operation after cooldown their motor breakers are opened/removed to prevent inadvertent closure, and MCR/remote-shutdown alarms indicate RCS pressure above RNS design pressure after the valves are open. The same historical passage says the four MOVs were powered from 125-Vdc safety-grade buses; that voltage statement is **superseded** by the later 125→250-Vdc design change controlled in `SRC-0467`/CHG-009 and must not be used for current Unit-3 source voltage. Use this source for interlock topology/diversity/operational-control history only pending current Unit-3 logic reconciliation. |
| SRC-0477 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-SHUTDOWN-RISK-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial FSER, Chapter 19, shutdown/severe-accident evaluation of RNS/IRWST cross-connect controls | Chapter 19 pp.19-119 through 19-120 and shutdown-risk discussion of RNS V023/V024 | NRC identifies the two remotely operated MOVs connecting the RNS suction and discharge headers to the IRWST as interlocked with the hot-leg suction isolation valves to prevent diversion/draining of the RCS during shutdown cooling. Do not import European A/B-suffixed V023/V024 variants into the U.S. Unit-3 model. |
