| SRC-0714 | B/A | AP1000-WESTINGHOUSE-RNS-SUCTION-SPATIAL-BASIS | Westinghouse AP1000 Pre-Construction Safety Report `UKR-GW-GL-793NP` Rev.1 plus NRC NUREG-1793 Chapter 5 RNS evaluation | Westinghouse Rev.1 Sections 10/17; NUREG-1793 Chapter 5 pp.5-80/81 | Westinghouse describes the RNS common suction header branching into two parallel lines, each with two normally closed motor-operated RCS pressure-boundary valves in series, and states that the valve in each pair **closest to the containment penetration** also serves as a containment-isolation valve. The component description explicitly places inner valves `RNS-PL-V001A/B` inside containment and identifies `RNS-PL-V002A/B` as the outer hot-leg suction/containment-isolation MOV family. NRC independently defines the RCPB portion as extending through the outer V002 valves and identifies V002A/B as both RCPB and containment-isolation valves. Carry this as a spatial/function constraint: V001 is the inner series valve; V002 is the outer series valve nearest the suction penetration. It does not supply the Unit-3 penetration number, valve-to-shell distance, centerline coordinates, intervening spool dimensions, welds, supports, or exact site isometric. Do not convert `closest to the containment penetration` into `immediately adjacent` or invent whether any particular local spool lies entirely inside or outside the containment shell. |
