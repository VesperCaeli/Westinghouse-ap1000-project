| SRC-0465 | A | AP1000/VOGTLE-DESIGN-HISTORY | NRC AP1000 design-document review of safety-related RNS valve requirements against the PV01 motor-operated-valve specification | ADAMS ML112440588, design-document review narrative and reviewed-document list | NRC states that inspectors evaluated safety-related valve functional/design requirements and compared `APP-PV01-Z0-001`, *3 in. and Larger Motor Operated Gate and Globe Valves, ASME Section III Classes 1, 2 and 3*, against `APP-RNS-M3C-100`, *RNS Component Control Requirements*, to verify consistency. The same inspection identifies `APP-RNS-M3C-050` as the MOV functional-requirements document specifically covering RNS hot-leg suction isolation valves V001A/B and V002A/B. | SRC-0466 | A | AP1000/VOGTLE-QUALIFICATION-HISTORY | NRC seismic equipment-qualification inspection of Normal Residual Heat Removal System MOVs | ADAMS ML17226A034, ITAAC 2.3.06.05a.ii inspection, PDF p.41 and reviewed-document list | NRC explicitly identifies `RNS-PL-V001A`, the RCS inner hot-leg suction motor-operated isolation valve, as commodity `PV01`. For that RNS sample the inspectors reviewed `APP-PV01-VBR-011`, *Equipment Qualification Summary Report for Flowserve Flex Wedge Gate Valves with Limitorque Motor Operators*, and `APP-PV01-VBR-012`, the corresponding qualification data package. This is direct tag-specific evidence that one member of the V001/V002 hot-leg suction MOV set belongs to the Flowserve/Limitorque PV01 qualification family. It materially strengthens `APP-PV01-Z0D-116` / Flowserve-Limitorque as the candidate subfamily for the four V001/V002 valves, but it is **not** transferred to `RNS-PL-V002B`; paired/sibling inference remains prohibited until a V002B-specific current/as-built or tag-to-datasheet record is recovered. |
