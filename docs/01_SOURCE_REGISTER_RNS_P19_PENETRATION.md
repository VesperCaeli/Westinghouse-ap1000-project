This supplement shares the global controlled `SRC-*` namespace with the other `01_SOURCE_REGISTER*.md` files. It corrects the earlier conceptual treatment of the V001B/V002B region by separating the B-side RCS-pressure-boundary valve branch from the **common RNS suction containment penetration**.

| SRC-0733 | A | AP1000-US-RNS-SUCTION-HEADER-SPATIAL-TOPOLOGY | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial Safety Evaluation Report, Chapter 5, RNS design evaluation | NUREG-1793 Chapter 5, §5.4.7.2 discussion, pp.5-80 through 5-87 | NRC describes the U.S. AP1000 suction architecture as follows: inside containment, a single hot-leg/step-nozzle connection feeds a suction arrangement containing **two parallel lines**, each with two normally closed series MOVs forming the RCS pressure boundary; the two lines then connect to a **common suction header**. Once outside containment, that common header contains a single normally closed motor-operated isolation valve; downstream of it the header splits into separate pump-A and pump-B suction lines, each with a normally open manual pump-maintenance isolation valve upstream of its pump. The SER further states that the portions from the RCS pressure-boundary valves to the containment-isolation valves outside containment are Safety Class B / ASME Section III Class 2 and that the inside-containment portions extending to the outside containment-isolation valves are designed for **full RCS pressure**. Carry this as the controlling U.S. topology and pressure-envelope correction: P19 is on the common RNS suction path after the V001/V002 parallel RCS-boundary branches have recombined, and the segment from the downstream side of the Class-1 RCPB isolation boundary through P19 to the outside containment-isolation valve must not be modeled as ordinary lower-pressure downstream RNS piping. This source does not print the intermediate line numbers, exact class-break weld, wall schedule, materials, dimensions, or physical valve spacing; those require current Unit-3 P&ID/line-table/isometric and construction records. |
