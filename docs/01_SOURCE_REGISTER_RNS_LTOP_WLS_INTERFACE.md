# Public Source Register Supplement — RNS LTOP to WLS Containment-Sump Interface

This supplement shares the global controlled `SRC-*` namespace. It defines the receiving-system boundary for the final Vogtle dual-LTOP relief subsystem without inventing a process-piping bridge between RNS relief-valve outlets and WLS sump-discharge hardware.

| Source ID | Authority | Configuration | Document | Locator / accession | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-0742 | A | VOGTLE-UFSAR-WLS-CONTAINMENT-SUMP-EQUIPMENT | VEGP 3&4 UFSAR Revision 8, Appendix 3I safety-related equipment population | ADAMS `ML19171A061`, Appendix 3I | The Vogtle UFSAR identifies `WLS-PL-V055` as **Sump Containment Isolation IRC**, `WLS-PL-V057` as **Sump Containment Isolation ORC**, and `WLS-PL-V058` as **WLS Containment Isolation Relief**. Carry these as real final-design WLS containment-sump boundary components. This source does not show the RNS V020/V021 relief discharge piping connected to any one of these valves; do not insert them into the LTOP relief outlet path without a P&ID/isometric bridge. |
| SRC-0743 | A | VOGTLE-3-WLS-SUMP-DISCHARGE-VALVE-CONSTRUCTION | NRC Unit-3 ASME construction inspection of WLS containment-isolation piping | ADAMS `ML19135A691`, Section 1A08 | NRC directly reviewed Unit-3 `SV3-WLS-PL-V055`, including an Aecon ASME Form N-5 for **Crane Nuclear Inc. valve S/N E2267**, piping subassemblies and a support installation. The same inspection identifies `SV3-WLS-M6-001` Rev.3, *Piping and Instrumentation Diagram Liquid Radwaste System*, and `SV3-Q305-WLS-PLW-010-00` Rev.4. Use this as the strongest current public Unit-3 drawing/isometric route for the WLS containment-sump discharge boundary and as a direct installed-hardware anchor for V055. It does not prove that the RNS LTOP relief outlet passes through V055; V055 is on the sump **discharge** containment-isolation path. |
| SRC-0744 | A | VOGTLE-3-WLS-SUMP-DISCHARGE-ACTIVE-FUNCTION | Southern Nuclear / NRC Unit-3 containment-isolation ITAAC completion records | ADAMS `ML19249C320`, `ML19266A636`, and `ML22115A248` | Unit-3 completion evidence identifies `WLS-PL-V055` and `WLS-PL-V057` as the sump-discharge containment-isolation IRC/ORC valves with active function **Transfer Closed** and required closure time no greater than 60 seconds. Carry these as the controlled WLS outlet/isolation function downstream of the containment sump. They are not LTOP relief isolation valves and must not be placed between V020/V021 and the sump based only on shared WLS ownership. |
| SRC-0745 | B | AP1000-WESTINGHOUSE-WLS-CONTAINMENT-SUMP-COMPONENT-IDENTITY | Westinghouse AP1000 European Design Control Document `EPS-GW-GL-700` Rev.1, §2.3.10 | Tables 2.3.10-1, -4 and -5 | The generic AP1000 WLS construction-verification basis identifies the **WLS containment sump as `WLS-MT-02`, located in containment**, and identifies containment-sump level channels `WLS-LT-034`, `WLS-LT-035`, and `WLS-LT-036`. It also describes the containment sump as a WLS receiving/storage component whose contents are pumped out for liquid-radwaste processing. Carry `WLS-MT-02` and the three level channels as generic standard-design identities pending current Vogtle tag/drawing reconciliation. Do not promote the generic tag or level-channel arrangement to exact Unit-3 as-built status without the current `SV3-WLS-M6-001`/equipment records. |
