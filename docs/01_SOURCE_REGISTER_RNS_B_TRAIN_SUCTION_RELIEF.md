| SRC-0712 | A | AP1000-CERTIFICATION-RNS-LTOP-DESIGN-BASIS | U.S. NRC NUREG-1793 Initial Safety Evaluation Report, Chapter 5, RNS/LTOP evaluation | NUREG-1793 Chapter 5 RNS design-description discussion and detailed LTOP evaluation | NRC's AP1000 certification evaluation identifies the RNS hot-leg suction relief valve `V021` / RNS suction relief valve as the LTOP relief valve and places the relief-valve connection on the RNS suction header inside containment. **The same Chapter 5 contains conflicting discharge-destination text:** the RNS design-description passage states that the single safety/relief valve discharges to the **IRWST**, while the later detailed LTOP evaluation states that the RNS suction relief valve discharges to the **containment sump**. Do not use the earlier project phrase `containment sump/containment atmosphere`; **containment atmosphere is not supported by the cited Chapter-5 passages.** |
