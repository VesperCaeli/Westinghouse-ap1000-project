# Public Source Register Supplement — Unit 3 RNS Post-LAR Geometry Control

This supplement shares the global controlled `SRC-*` namespace. It records the strongest public drawing-control evidence currently available for reconstructing the final Unit-3 RNS suction/common-header geometry after the plant-specific LTOP change, while keeping actual drawing contents and as-built isometric geometry unresolved until recovered.

| Source ID | Authority | Configuration | Document | Locator / accession | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-0759 | A | VOGTLE-3-RNS-SITE-PID-FA-2020 | NRC Vogtle Unit-3 integrated construction / functional-arrangement inspection | ADAMS `ML21041A051`, Section 1A01 reviewed-document list | NRC directly identifies **`SV3-RNS-M6-001` Revision 4**, *Piping and Instrumentation Diagram Normal Residual Heat Removal System*, and **`SV3-RNS-M6K-FA201` Revision 0**, the Unit-3 RNS functional-arrangement sketch derived from that P&ID. They were reviewed together with the Unit-3 RCS/PXS/CVS drawings during the integrated functional-arrangement inspection. Carry these as the strongest public Unit-3 site drawing-control anchors for final tag-to-line topology. The public inspection report prints the drawing identities/revisions but not their actual symbols, line numbers, coordinates, branch fittings, valve spacing or isometric cross-references. |
| SRC-0760 | A | AP1000-RNS-STANDARD-PID-2020-RECONCILIATION | NRC Vogtle Unit-3 integrated construction / hydrostatic-test inspection | ADAMS `ML21041A051`, Section 1A04 reviewed-document list | The same NRC inspection directly identifies **`APP-RNS-M6-001` Revision 13**, *Piping and Instrumentation Diagram Normal Residual Heat Removal System*, within the standard-design/current-design document population used for the Unit-3 review. The inspection also identifies Unit-3 cold-hydrostatic-test work package `SV3-RCS-THW-1013872` / `SV3-TH-H8001A` as covering RCS, PXS, CVS and RNS lines. Use APP Rev.13 as a design-reconciliation source against the site-specific `SV3-RNS-M6-001 Rev.4`; do not substitute APP Rev.13 for the Unit-3 as-built drawing or assume every standard-design revision/change was implemented identically on Unit 3 without the site change-control bridge. |
