# Public Source Register Supplement — RNS P19 Common-Header Interfaces

This supplement shares the global controlled `SRC-*` namespace. It records plant-specific/common-header interfaces around penetration P19 without collapsing the V001/V002 branch, V022 common-header isolation, V061 CVS-return branch, or LTOP relief branches into one physical node.

Use this as direct Vogtle configuration-control evidence that the V061 containment/class-break interface is associated with the P19 Tier-1 arrangement. It does **not** prove that V061 shares a tee, spool, or penetration sleeve with V002A/B, V022, V020, or V021; recover the post-LAR P&ID/isometric/Tier-1 figure before assigning exact local connectivity or coordinates. |
| SRC-0751 | A | VOGTLE-3-RNS-V061-FUNCTION-AND-PST | Unit-3 RNS EQ/PST and UFSAR equipment records | ADAMS `ML16307A430` Attachment A; `ML21133A105` PST inspection; `ML19171A061` Appendix 3I | Unit-3 records identify `RNS-PL-V061` as the **CVS Return to RNS Suction / in-containment containment-isolation valve**. UFSAR equipment records identify child tags `RNS-PL-V061-L` (limit switch) and `RNS-PL-V061-S` (air operator), while DCD auxiliary-system tables describe V061 as normally closed / fail closed for shutdown-purification-flow isolation. Carry V061 as a separate CVS-return branch/interface into the RNS suction system, with an air-operated containment-isolation function. The public records do not expose its exact tie-in line, valve size/model/serial, air-supply components, local tubing, positioner/solenoids, penetration-side spool, or exact relationship to P19 beyond the Tier-1 class-break association in `SRC-0750`. |
